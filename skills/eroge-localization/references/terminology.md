# Terminology reference

Reusable knowledge for Japanese eroge → Simplified Chinese. These are semantic contrasts and possible Chinese renderings, not replacement rules. A slash groups related forms or alternatives; it does not assert equivalence. Choose the current sense and voice from the title's text. Record only title-specific adopted forms, exceptions, names, and unresolved choices in the [title glossary](../assets/templates/title-glossary.md); keep this shared reference in the skill package.

## Research basis and limits

Sources were checked on 2026-09-30. Shogakukan dictionary entries reproduced on Kotobank supply the dictionary editors' own lexical definitions; Shueisha's imidas supplies publisher-hosted dictionary entries. Official engine documentation supplies function meanings. Clinical and support organizations supply narrowly stated anatomy or consent distinctions. Chinese renderings and localization cautions are editorial recommendations, not wording mandated by those sources.

An industry's authored glossary is evidence of its usage, not authority for physiology, identity, or every game's setting. Fantasy labels and game statistics take their operative meaning from the title. This curated reference covers frequent ambiguities; it is not an exhaustive lexicon or a clinical taxonomy.

## Applying a term

Read the complete expression first, then identify its category, referent, register, and sense. Use an existing title decision only under its recorded conditions. A new recurring mechanism, name, or special sense belongs in the title glossary with an occurrence and explanation; a routine contextual wording choice can remain in the translation.

Keep separate forms for a formal name, alias, nickname, and anonymous label when speakers or disclosure stages differ. A confirmed common referent links those forms; it does not make them globally interchangeable. [Text and context](text-and-context.md) covers viewpoint, naming, sound treatment, and complete language review.

When sources disagree, preserve the distinction they actually establish. A dictionary can show multiple senses; an industry glossary can show a label in use; only the title can establish its fictional mechanism or its chosen parameter definition. Record a consequential uncertainty with the Japanese occurrence and competing meanings instead of silently upgrading a guess into a glossary decision.
