# Independent Reader Check

Before publishing new or substantially changed instructions, decisions, or handoffs that others will act on, use a fresh-context subagent. Self-review ordinary short replies.

Start the subagent with only the draft, destination, intended audience, the project's collaboration rules, and references available to that audience. Have it read as a recipient encountering the material for the first time.

Ask the reader to explain their understanding and the next action, then identify missing context, ambiguous references, unsupported conclusions, and information outside the sharing scope. Correct substantive findings before publishing. Verify factual claims against their evidence and follow the task's publication authorization separately.

If a fresh-context subagent is unavailable, self-review without claiming independent review.
